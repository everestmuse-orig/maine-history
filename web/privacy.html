Privacy Policy — Mainely History Uploader

Effective October 27, 2026

The Mainely History Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by Jonathan Feeley (Digital Interactif) to publish and manage videos on the Mainely History YouTube channel. It is not a public service and has no users other than its operator.

What data the app accesses

Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API, the app accesses the operator's own Google/YouTube account for one purpose: uploading videos, setting their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and playlists, and reading back their status on the operator's own channel. The app does not access any other person's Google account or data.

How the data is used

Account access is used solely to publish and manage the operator's own YouTube content. No Google user data is sold, rented, shared, or transferred to any third party, and none is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the channel.

Storage and retention

The app's OAuth access token is stored locally on the operator's own computer. The app keeps no server-side database of Google user data. Uploaded video files and their metadata are the operator's own content, stored in the operator's own accounts.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the operator's Google Account security settings (Third-party access), which immediately stops the app from reaching the account. Locally stored tokens are deleted on revocation.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Mainely History Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
